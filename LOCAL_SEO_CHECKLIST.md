```
[Nurse Name] & Team DressingWala

```

### Review Response Strategy

**5-Star Reviews**:
```
Thank you so much, [Name]! 🙏 We're thrilled that [Nurse Name] provided excellent care. Your comfort and recovery are our top priorities. We're here whenever you need us again. - Team DressingWala
```

**4-Star Reviews**:
```
Thank you for your feedback, [Name]! We're glad we could help, and we appreciate you pointing out [specific issue]. We're working to improve this. Please reach out if there's anything more we can do. - Team DressingWala
```

**3-Star or Lower**:
```
We're sorry we didn't meet your expectations, [Name]. Your feedback is important to us. Could we discuss this directly? Please call/WhatsApp us at [number] so we can make this right. - [Manager Name], DressingWala
```

**Response Rules**:
- Respond within 24 hours
- Always thank the reviewer
- Address specific points mentioned
- Keep it professional and empathetic
- Offer to resolve issues offline
- Never argue or be defensive
- Always sign with a name
